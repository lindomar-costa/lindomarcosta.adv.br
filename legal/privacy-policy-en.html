Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy aims to clarify how my institutional law firm website collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal data of visitors, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law No. 13.709/2018) and aligned with global standards such as the GDPR.

By browsing this site, you agree to the practices described in this document.

1. Information Collected

The site is purely institutional and informational. It does not have registration forms, comment boxes, or account creation systems. Data collection occurs in two ways:

  • a) Data provided voluntarily by you (via Email): When you contact me through the official email links provided on the website, you will voluntarily share your email address, name, and any other information you decide to include in your message for the purpose of legal consultation.
  • b) Automatically collected data (Cookies and Analytics): I use Google tools (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with the site and to optimize my content. These tools may automatically collect data such as:
    • IP address;
    • Browser type and operating system;
    • Pages visited on the site, time spent, and clicks;
    • Approximate location data (city/state level).

2. Use of Information

The collected information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Legal Assistance: The data you share via email is used strictly so that I can respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, and provide the requested legal services, fully protected by attorney-client privilege.
  • Site Improvement: Automated data (Analytics) serves to measure website audience, understand visitor behavior, and optimize user experience. None of this automatic information is used to identify you personally.

3. Data Sharing with Third Parties

I do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to third parties. Sharing only occurs with:

  • Google (Analytics): For processing traffic metrics and website performance.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or applicable regulation.

4. Storage and Security

Contact data provided via email is stored securely and protected by the strict confidentiality inherent to the attorney-client relationship. Browsing data processed by Google is maintained according to the platform's own retention policies.

5. Your Data Subject Rights

You have full control over your data. In compliance with LGPD and GDPR frameworks, you can request at any time:

  • Confirmation of the existence of data processing;
  • Access to the data I hold about you;
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • Deletion of personal data (such as email communication history), except when maintenance is necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

6. Cookie Management

You can disable cookie collection at any time through your browser settings or by using private browsing tabs. Note that this will not prevent your navigation on the site.

7. Contact

To exercise your privacy rights or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me directly:
