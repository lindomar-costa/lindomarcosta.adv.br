Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy aims to clarify how this institutional website collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal data of visitors, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018 — LGPD). By browsing the site, you agree to the practices described in this document.

1. Information Collected

The site is purely institutional and informational. It has no registration forms, comment sections, or account creation systems. Data collection occurs in two ways:

a) Data voluntarily provided (via Email)

When you click on the email contact links on the site, you are redirected to your email client. Any information you choose to share (such as your name and email address) will be used for consultation or legal service purposes.

b) Automatically collected data (Cookies & Analytics)

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the site. These tools may automatically collect:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type and operating system;
  • Pages visited, time spent, and clicks;
  • Approximate location data (city/state level).

2. Use of Information

Legal Services: Data shared via email is used exclusively to respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and provide legal services, protected by professional confidentiality.

Site Improvement: Automated data is used to measure site traffic and understand visitor behavior. None of this information is used to personally identify you.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

There is no sale, rental, or transfer of personal data to third parties. Sharing occurs only with:

Google (Analytics): For traffic metrics processing.

Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or applicable regulation.

Third-party privacy policies:

Google Privacy Policy

4. Storage and Security

Contact data provided via email is stored securely and protected by the attorney-client privilege. Navigation data processed by Google is maintained according to the platform's own retention policies.

5. Your Rights (LGPD/GDPR)

You have full control over your data. You may request at any time:

  • → Confirmation of whether your data is being processed;
  • → Access to the data I hold about you;
  • → Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • → Deletion of your personal data, except where retention is required by law.

6. Cookie Management

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings or by using private browsing tabs. This will not prevent you from navigating the site.
